About

I graduated from the American University of Beirut with a degree in computer and communication engineering. My work focuses on large language models, reasoning transfer, and model merging.

Previously, I worked as a research assistant with the Image and Video Understanding Lab at KAUST and with Edgebot's post-training team. I have also co-instructed courses at AUB on machine learning and AI in medicine.

I recently joined a small team of very smart people to work on something new in research. We are currently in stealth mode.

This is where I write up what I am learning. Read my research notes.
